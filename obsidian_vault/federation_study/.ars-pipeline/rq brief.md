**Primary research question:**

> How can resource planning be optimized in an archipelagic nation that adopts a federal system of government — which mechanisms of intergovernmental resource distribution best correct the mainland–island fiscal gap, and which failure modes must the design guard against?

**Sub-questions (mapped to vault note skeleton):**

**Scope:**

- In: fiscal federalism theory; transfer/equalization design; archipelagic fiscal constraints (Indonesia as primary evidence base); documented failure modes (Pakistan, Sudan, Ethiopia, Nigeria); optimization principles for formula design.
- Out: confederal arrangements; monetary policy; primary data collection.
- Honesty constraint (from corpus gap analysis): no non-Indonesian archipelagic federation exists in the corpus; Indonesia is formally unitary with asymmetric features. The paper treats Indonesia as the archipelagic-fiscal evidence base and reasons by explicit analogy to a hypothetical archipelagic federation. This limitation is stated, not hidden.
